Privacy Notice

Last updated: 9 November 2026

CyberShield UK is a pre-launch cybersecurity business. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling personal information responsibly.

1. Information we collect

When you complete our contact form, we collect your name, email address, selected service and any information you include in your message.

2. How we use your information

We use this information to respond to enquiries, provide information about our proposed services when requested, and manage correspondence.

3. Legal basis

We generally process enquiry information under our legitimate interests in responding to communications, or to take steps requested before entering into a contract, where applicable.

4. Email delivery

Our contact form uses FormSubmit, a third-party service that processes and forwards submitted information to our business email address. Please avoid submitting passwords, financial details or sensitive personal information.

5. Sharing information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by our website hosting, email and form delivery providers as necessary to operate the website and respond to enquiries.

6. Data retention

We retain enquiry correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary for responding, follow-up and applicable legal requirements. A specific retention schedule will be established before commercial launch.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information, depending on the circumstances.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

8. Cookies and hosting

We do not currently operate our own advertising or analytics cookies. Hosting and third-party service providers may process technical information required to operate their services.

9. Contact us

10. Before commercial launch

This notice will be reviewed and expanded before paid services launch, including confirmation of the legal business identity, data controller details, supplier arrangements, retention periods and any international data transfers.
